02 / 03 Capabilities

Six capability pillars.

Key figures shown are representative of our standard processes — project-specific tolerances are quoted per engagement.

C·01

Electron-Beam Lithography

Direct-write patterning for master templates, photomasks and ultra-fine features on 100 mm wafers.

CD ≤ 8 nm [SAMPLE] · overlay < 30 nm

C·02

Thin-Film Deposition

ALD and PVD stacks with sub-monolayer thickness control for optical, electrical and barrier layers.

±0.1 nm control · <1% wafer uniformity [SAMPLE]

C·03

Reactive-Ion Etching

Anisotropic, low-damage plasma etching of silicon, dielectrics and polymers with vertical profiles.

aspect ratio > 20:1 [SAMPLE] · CD-loss < 5 nm

C·04

SEM & AFM Metrology

Critical-dimension SEM, topographic AFM and film-thickness mapping with calibration traceability.

1 nm SEM resolution · ISO-traceable cal [SAMPLE]

C·05

Multiphysics Simulation

FEM and molecular-dynamics modeling of process windows, actuation, thermal and fluidic behavior.

model-to-measurement error < 8% [SAMPLE]

C·06

Wafer-Level Packaging

Bonding, dicing, and die-level integration with electrical screening before anything leaves the lab.

100% device screening · class-1000 environment [SAMPLE]
